## Methods

### Session Detection Algorithm

```python
SESSION_GAP_THRESHOLD = 1.0  # 60 minutes

for i, gap in enumerate(gaps):
    if gap >= SESSION_GAP_THRESHOLD:
        session_starts.append(i + 1)  # New session
```

### Statistical Tests

- **Effect size:** Cohen's d
- **Significance:** Mann-Whitney U test (non-parametric)
- **Threshold:** p < 0.05

## Key Results

### Top Discriminating Features (by Cohen's d)

| Feature | Aprobados | Reprobados | Ratio | Cohen's d | p-value |
|---------|-----------|------------|-------|-----------|---------|
| Total Sesiones | 39.5 | 21.4 | 1.85x | **0.82** (LARGE) | 4.77e-17 *** |
| Horas Únicas Activas | 39.5 | 21.4 | 1.85x | **0.82** (LARGE) | 4.77e-17 *** |
| Participaciones | 6.6 | 2.7 | 2.49x | **0.78** (LARGE) | 3.58e-18 *** |
| Sesiones/Semana | 1.80 | 1.06 | 1.69x | 0.69 | 3.29e-12 *** |
| Page Views | 556 | 309 | 1.80x | 0.63 | 3.47e-13 *** |
| Gap Medio (horas) | 85 | 139 | 0.61x | -0.61 | 4.13e-09 *** |

### Key Insights

1. **Session count is the strongest predictor** (d=0.82) - approved students have 1.85x more sessions
2. **Participations show 2.49x difference** - the highest ratio among all features
3. **Gap between sessions** - approved wait 54 hours less between visits
4. **Procrastination pattern** - failing students show "strong start, early abandonment"
